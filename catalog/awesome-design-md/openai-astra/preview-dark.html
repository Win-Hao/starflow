Rules

Do

  • Keep the canvas #000000; the glow supplies softness.
  • 676px copy column, empty rails on both sides.
  • Weight 500 headings, 400 body, -0.03em display tracking.
  • Only stars respond to scroll.
  • Ship the static fallback; honour reduced motion.

Don't

  • No gradients, coloured buttons, tinted cards.
  • No serif, second sans, 600+, italics.
  • No parallax on copy, pinned sections, scroll-snap.
  • No shadows or backdrop-filter over the canvas.
  • No affiliation claims.
